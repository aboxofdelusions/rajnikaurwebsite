About Rajni Kaur

Rajni Kaur was born in Delhi, India, and lives in Dublin, Ireland. She has written poetry for fourteen years, exploring women’s experiences, emotional intensity, mental health, and the quiet absurdity of society’s expectations. Inspired by Sufi and spiritual poets, especially Rumi, she uses poetry to preserve softness and transform powerful emotions into words. Box of Delusions is her debut collection. Through her writing, Rajni hopes readers find comfort, companionship, and the reassurance that they are not alone.
